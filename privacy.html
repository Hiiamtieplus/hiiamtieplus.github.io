Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 (version 3). Replaces version 2 (effective 28 September 2026).

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cong Ty Kenh (the "tool") accesses when its owner connects the owner's YouTube channels, and how that information is used, stored, shared and deleted. Cong Ty Kenh is a private, single-user desktop tool: its only user is its owner, and it only manages YouTube channels that the owner owns or manages. From version 3 the tool can also upload videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own channels, in Private mode only (see sections 4 and 5). You must agree to this policy before using the tool.

2. YouTube API Services and Google

Cong Ty Kenh uses YouTube API Services. When you use the tool, Google and YouTube also process your data under the Google Privacy Policy: http://www.google.com/policies/privacy. Your use of YouTube through the tool is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

3. Information the tool accesses

  • Channel information: channel ID, title and handle.
  • Channel statistics: subscriber count, view count, video count, and whether the subscriber count is hidden.
  • Channel branding settings: the channel description and keywords. The current text is read before any change so that the change can be undone. If the channel has a banner image, its reference is sent back unchanged whenever branding settings are updated, so that the banner is not removed.
  • Channel settings: the channel's country and default language. They are read, and set only for a channel whose profile the owner has approved: the country is set to Vietnam, where the owner lives, and the default language to the channel's language. The current values are read before any change so that the change can be undone.
  • Channel status (read only): whether the channel can upload videos longer than 15 minutes (which shows whether phone verification is done), whether the channel is set as made for kids, and whether it has a banner image. The tool never changes these.
  • Playlists: the titles of the owner's own playlists, read to avoid creating the same playlist twice. Playlist IDs are not stored.
  • Video uploads (new in version 3): the tool sends to YouTube a video file that the owner has produced and approved, together with its title, description, tags, category, default language and thumbnail image. From YouTube it receives only the new video's ID, its privacy setting, its channel and whether it was declared as synthetic content, which the tool reads back to confirm the upload. Before any upload the tool checks that the authorized channel is the owner's own channel. For a YouTube Short, the tool inserts into the description a link to the owner's own full-length video on the same channel. Uploads use the same single permission the owner already granted (manage the owner's YouTube account); no new permission is requested.
  • OAuth authorization: the refresh token that Google issues when the owner grants access.

The tool does not access or store Google passwords, emails, contacts, payment information, or data about other people's accounts.

4. How the information is used

  • To show the owner a private dashboard of the owner's own channels.
  • To update a channel's description (and, if chosen, keywords), country and default language to match the channel profile the owner has reviewed and approved, and to read them back to confirm the change.
  • To create public playlists whose titles the owner has approved in the channel profile. The tool never deletes or renames playlists.
  • To show the owner which setup steps are still missing for each channel (for example, phone verification).
  • To upload videos that the owner has produced to the owner's own channel in Private mode only. The tool always sets the video's privacy status to Private and has no setting that can change this, so it can never publish a video as Public or Unlisted. Only the owner can change a video's visibility, by hand in YouTube Studio.
  • To keep a short local history of changes so that the owner can undo a change.

The information is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles.

5. AI-generated content

  • Some of the video content that the tool uploads is generated or assisted by artificial intelligence: video clips and still images made with Google Flow (using the owner's own Google accounts, separate from any YouTube channel), synthetic narration, and computer-generated graphics.
  • Every upload is marked as synthetic: the tool turns on YouTube's altered-or-synthetic-content declaration and adds an AI-disclosure sentence to the video description. The tool refuses to upload a video if either of the two is missing.
  • Clips made in Google Flow keep Google's own watermarks, including the invisible SynthID mark and, on some plans, a visible one. The tool does not remove, hide or cover them.
  • The channel information obtained through the owner's YouTube authorization (section 3) is not sent to Google Flow or to any other AI service.

6. Where it is stored and how it is protected

  • Everything is stored only on the owner's personal Windows computer. The tool has no server, and no copy is kept anywhere else.
  • The OAuth authorization is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and can only be decrypted by the owner's Windows account on that computer.
  • Information leaves the computer only to Google and YouTube, always over HTTPS: to call YouTube API Services (including the video uploads described above) and, for AI-generated media, to use Google Flow.

7. Sharing

The information is not shared with any third party, internal or external. The only party that receives it is Google/YouTube, when the tool calls YouTube API Services. The tool does not show advertisements and does not allow third parties to serve content or advertisements.

8. Cookies and data on your device

Cong Ty Kenh is a desktop tool, not a website. It stores the files described in this policy on the owner's own computer: the encrypted authorization, cached channel statistics, a short change history, small markers (a hash of a playlist title and a time) used to avoid creating the same playlist twice, and the information returned by YouTube after an upload. It does not place cookies or similar technology in any browser, and it does not collect information from anyone else's device. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not set cookies; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. How long data is kept

  • The OAuth authorization is kept until the owner disconnects the channel in the tool or revokes access.
  • All other YouTube data stored on the computer (channel statistics, the change history and backups used for undo, playlist creation markers, the video ID and other information returned after an upload, and log entries that contain channel text) is kept for no longer than 30 days; after that it is deleted or refreshed. The tool's own upload log records only which file was sent, when, and whether the upload finished; it contains no data returned by YouTube. Undo is therefore available only for changes made within the last 30 days.
  • The videos, images, audio and caption files that the owner produces are the owner's own files, not data obtained from YouTube. They stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them, and once uploaded they are the owner's own content on YouTube.

10. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Cong Ty Kenh's access to your YouTube data at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also available at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). In addition, the owner can disconnect a channel inside the tool, which deletes the stored authorization, and can delete all YouTube data that the tool stores on the computer. After access is revoked, related YouTube data stored on the computer is deleted within 7 days.

11. Changes to this policy

Version 3 describes the upload of videos to the owner's own channels, which version 2 announced as planned. The tool does not upload any video until this version is published and the owner has accepted it. If the tool starts to access or use data in another new way (for example, publishing videos as Public or Unlisted, or passing YouTube data to an AI service), this policy will be updated before that feature is used, and the updated policy must be accepted again. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact

Questions or complaints about the tool's privacy practices: hiiamtieplus@gmail.com.

Cong Ty Kenh dùng YouTube API Services; dữ liệu còn chịu Chính sách quyền riêng tư của Google. Từ bản 3, công cụ còn tải lên kênh của chính chủ các video do chủ làm ra (kèm tiêu đề, mô tả, thẻ, ảnh thumbnail; với Short, công cụ chèn liên kết tới video dài của chính kênh vào mô tả), dùng đúng quyền chủ đã cấp — không xin quyền mới, CHỈ ở chế độ Riêng tư: công cụ luôn đặt Riêng tư và không có tuỳ chọn nào đổi sang Công khai hay Không công khai (chỉ chủ tự đổi trong YouTube Studio). Một phần nội dung video do AI tạo (hình và đoạn phim tạo bằng Google Flow, giọng đọc tổng hợp, đồ hoạ máy tính); mỗi video đều được khai là nội dung tổng hợp với YouTube và có câu công bố AI trong mô tả, thiếu một trong hai thì công cụ không tải lên; hình mờ của Google (kể cả SynthID) được giữ nguyên, không che. Thông tin kênh lấy qua quyền YouTube không được gửi cho Google Flow hay dịch vụ AI nào. Không truy cập mật khẩu, email, danh bạ hay dữ liệu người khác. Mọi thứ chỉ lưu trên máy tính Windows của chủ; giấy phép mã hoá bằng Windows DPAPI; không máy chủ riêng; không chia sẻ cho bên thứ ba, không quảng cáo. Dữ liệu YouTube lưu trên máy (trừ giấy phép) không quá 30 ngày rồi xoá hoặc làm mới. Có thể thu hồi quyền bất cứ lúc nào tại trang cài đặt bảo mật của Google; sau khi thu hồi, dữ liệu liên quan trên máy được xoá trong 7 ngày. Liên hệ: hiiamtieplus@gmail.com.
